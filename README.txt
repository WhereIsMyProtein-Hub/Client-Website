WHERE IS MY PROTEIN? — PREMIUM CAFÉ WEBSITE DEMO

Open index.html in Chrome. Keep the assets folder beside index.html. Internet access is needed for Google Fonts and remote food photographs.

FEATURES
• Premium forest-green, orange and cream branding with café logo.
• Veg / Non-Veg menu filters, menu categories, search and nutrition estimates.
• Choose Dine-in, Takeaway or Home Delivery before browsing/checkout.
• Login / Sign-up demo prompt before a customer can add a menu item.
• Add multiple items without being redirected to Your Tray. Open Your Tray manually when ready.
• Coupon codes: FUEL10 (10%), FIT15 (15%), PROTEIN50 (₹50 off), POWER20 (20%).
• Dine-in table number; takeaway name and phone; delivery name, phone, full address, landmark, area, city, PIN and instructions.
• GST estimate, automatic New Order → Preparing → Ready → Served tracking, café-branded bill and automatic print dialog.
• Closing quote in footer: “Fuel your body, feed your goals, and enjoy every bite.”

DEMO LIMITATIONS
Login/sign-up is a front-end demonstration and does not create secure accounts or store passwords. Orders and tracking are simulated in the browser and are not sent to a kitchen or delivery partner. No real payment is collected. GST is shown as a demo 5% calculation. Reviews are session-only. Nutrition values and menu prices are estimates; verify all business, tax, allergen and menu information before commercial use.
